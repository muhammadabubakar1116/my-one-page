About the drafter.

I'm a BS Computer Science student at Sargodha University with a strong foundation in programming, web development and problem-solving. Semester after semester, I'm the person who volunteers to lead the project no one else wants to structure first.

I sit at the intersection of two crafts: I write HTML & CSS to build interfaces, and I use Figma, CorelDRAW, Photoshop and Canva to design them before a single line of code is written. That combination means what I ship usually looks the way it was meant to.

Outside of coursework, I lead teams, sketch layouts, and I'm always looking for the next thing worth learning — or the next place worth visiting.
